Governed metrics, each defined once by its owner (config/bi/metrics) and compiled to a query on its source; no metric tool takes SQL. Start with bi_list_metrics and bi_describe_metric. Periods: 2025-Q3, 2025-07, 2025, FY2025, YTD, QTD, MTD, last 12 months or {from, to}; relative ones end today. as_of is the latest date in the source. Ad-hoc SQL: duckdb_query or a data connection's tools.
