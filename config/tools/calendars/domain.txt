Holiday and business-day calendars computed offline from the rules of the holidays Python package (MIT licence); no network call and no key. A calendar is a country (ISO 3166, e.g. US), a subdivision (CA-ON) or a market (ISO 10383, e.g. XNYS, XLON, XTSE; XECB is TARGET2). Dates are YYYY-MM-DD. With several calendars a day is a business day only if it is one in all of them. Each result names its source.
