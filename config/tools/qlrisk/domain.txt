QLRisk pricing and risk analytics, computed in process by the optional qlrisk library; no API key or outside data. Units: a field ending _pct is in percent (3 = 3%), one ending _bp in basis points; maturities are a tenor (3Y, 18M, 6W, 10D) or a date YYYY-MM-DD. Without market data a tool uses qlrisk's sample market valued today. The pricing tools' optional `advanced` object takes trade_id, trade_date, counterparty_id, market_data, reporting_currency, include_cash_flows.
