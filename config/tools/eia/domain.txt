Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration (api.eia.gov), public domain; every result carries source. Key EIA_API_KEY; without it the shared DEMO_KEY (about ten requests a day for the whole organisation). Data are a tree of routes (petroleum/pri/spt) with facets and frequencies: eia_browse finds them, eia_series reads any leaf. period: 2025, 2025-07, 'last 30 days' or 2025-01-01:2025-06-30. fred_* re-publishes some of these series.
