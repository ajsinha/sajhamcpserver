GLEIF, the Global LEI Foundation (api.gleif.org): legal entity identifiers (LEIs) and corporate hierarchies. No API key. LEI data is CC0; every result carries a source field, and GLEIF's name implies no endorsement. An LEI is 20 characters: 18 letters or digits, then 2 check digits. Countries are ISO 3166 alpha-2. Statuses: entity ACTIVE or INACTIVE; registration ISSUED, LAPSED, RETIRED and others. Identifiers from a company name: wikidata_company_identifiers; SEC filers: edgar_company_search.
